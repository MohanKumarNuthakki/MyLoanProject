Step-by-Step Loan Application and Approval Process

Our Smart Loan Portal simplifies the loan approval process using AI and automation. Below is a complete overview of how your loan request moves from submission to disbursement.

1. Loan Application Submission

The applicant fills out the online loan application form with personal, employment, and financial details. All inputs are securely stored in the database for verification and processing.

2. Document Upload & Verification

Users upload required documents like ID proof, income proof, and address proof. The system performs basic document validation to ensure authenticity and completeness.

3. Credit Score Check

The system automatically retrieves the applicant’s credit score or history (if available) and evaluates their financial discipline for preliminary approval.

4. AI-Based Eligibility Prediction

The pre-trained AI model analyzes the user data (income, liabilities, employment, etc.) and predicts the loan eligibility, approval probability, and estimated interest rate.

5. RBI Compliance & Policy Check

The application is checked against RBI’s regulations and policies to ensure that the loan meets all mandatory financial guidelines and safety protocols.

6. Manual Review (if required)

In cases where the AI prediction is uncertain, the application is reviewed manually by an authorized admin or bank officer through the Admin Dashboard.

7. Approval / Rejection

Based on both AI prediction and policy checks, the system updates the loan status as Approved or Rejected in the database. Applicants are notified instantly.

8. Agreement & Digital Signature

If approved, the applicant must digitally sign the loan agreement that contains the terms, EMI schedule, and repayment obligations.

9. Loan Disbursement

After final approval and digital agreement, the loan amount is disbursed directly to the applicant’s registered bank account within a few business days.

10. Loan Tracking & EMI Payment

Users can track their loan status, repayment schedule, and due EMIs through the dashboard. Reminders and alerts are automatically sent for upcoming payments.
