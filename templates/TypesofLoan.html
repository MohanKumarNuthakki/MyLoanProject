Types of Loans

Different Types of Loans Offered by Banks

Banks and financial institutions offer a wide range of loan products to meet the diverse financial needs of individuals and businesses. Below are the most common types of loans with their purposes and brief explanations.

1. Personal Loan

Unsecured loan provided based on income and credit history. It can be used for personal expenses like travel, education, or medical emergencies.

2. Home Loan

Secured loan used to purchase or construct a house. Usually offers longer repayment periods and lower interest rates.

3. Vehicle Loan

Used to finance the purchase of a new or used car, bike, or other vehicle. The vehicle itself is the collateral for the loan.

4. Education Loan

Helps students cover tuition fees and related educational expenses. Repayment usually begins after course completion.

5. Business Loan

Provided to entrepreneurs for expanding or starting a business. May be secured or unsecured depending on the business size and credit record.

6. Gold Loan

Short-term loan given against pledged gold ornaments. Offers instant approval and minimal documentation.

7. Agricultural Loan

Designed for farmers to purchase seeds, fertilizers, or equipment. Often comes with government subsidies or lower interest rates.

8. Loan Against Property (LAP)

Secured loan where borrowers can mortgage their property to get a higher loan amount for business or personal use.

9. Credit Card Loan

Instant loan on a credit card with fixed EMI repayment option. Usually available to regular card users with good repayment history.

10. Microfinance Loan

Small loan offered to low-income individuals or small entrepreneurs who lack access to formal banking services.
