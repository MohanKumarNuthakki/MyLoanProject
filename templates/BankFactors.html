Key Internal Factors Considered by Banks During Loan Approval

Each bank evaluates multiple internal and external parameters before approving a loan. These factors determine the bank’s ability to manage risk, ensure profitability, and maintain regulatory compliance. Here are the most important internal factors affecting loan approval:

1. Bank’s Credit Policy

Every bank follows its own credit policy that outlines lending limits, eligibility rules, and documentation standards. These policies help maintain uniformity and reduce risk in loan approvals.

2. Risk Assessment Models

Banks use AI and statistical models to evaluate the probability of default for each applicant. This involves scoring based on income, repayment capacity, and credit history.

3. Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)

Regulations require banks to maintain a certain level of capital reserve. If this ratio is low, banks become more selective about approving high-value loans.

4. Liquidity Position

The bank’s available cash and liquid assets determine how much it can lend at a given time. A strong liquidity position allows for quicker approvals.

5. Loan Portfolio Performance

Banks review their existing loan portfolio to balance between high-risk and low-risk borrowers. High levels of non-performing assets (NPAs) can tighten approval policies.

6. Interest Rate Policy

Depending on the RBI repo rate and the bank’s cost of funds, each bank sets its own interest rate policy, which influences loan approval terms and conditions.

7. Branch Performance and Targets

Loan approvals can also depend on the branch’s performance metrics and monthly or quarterly lending targets set by head offices.

8. Customer Relationship History

Existing customers with good transaction records, savings, and previous loan repayment histories are preferred and often get faster approvals.

9. Collateral Evaluation Process

The internal team verifies and evaluates the collateral’s value and authenticity to ensure the loan is properly secured.

10. Compliance and Audit Controls

Banks ensure that every loan follows internal compliance checks, documentation verification, and audit rules before final approval.
