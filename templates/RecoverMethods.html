Loan Recovery Methods

Common Recovery Methods in Loan Applications

When a borrower fails to repay a loan, financial institutions use different methods to recover the due amount. These recovery methods ensure that the process remains fair, transparent, and compliant with RBI guidelines.

  • 1. Reminder Notices: The bank first sends polite reminders and follow-up messages to the borrower to clear the pending dues before the due date.
  • 2. Restructuring of Loan: The bank may provide relaxation by extending the tenure or reducing the EMI temporarily to help the borrower repay easily.
  • 3. Auto-Debit Recovery: For salaried or account-linked loans, the EMI amount can be automatically deducted from the borrower’s bank account on the due date.
  • 4. Legal Notice: If the borrower fails to respond after multiple reminders, a legal notice is issued as per RBI guidelines before taking further action.
  • 5. Asset Seizure: For secured loans (like home or car loans), the bank may recover the loan by seizing and auctioning the collateral asset.
  • 6. Credit Bureau Reporting: Loan defaults are reported to credit agencies like CIBIL, which affect the borrower’s credit score and future loan eligibility.
  • 7. Collection Agencies: In some cases, banks authorize third-party recovery agencies to collect overdue payments under ethical and legal limits.
  • 8. Settlement or One-Time Payment: The borrower can negotiate a one-time settlement with the lender by paying a lump-sum agreed amount.
  • 9. Legal Proceedings: If all previous methods fail, banks may initiate court proceedings to recover the outstanding loan amount legally.
  • 10. AI-Based Recovery Prediction: Using Artificial Intelligence, banks can now identify risky accounts early and provide customized recovery plans.
