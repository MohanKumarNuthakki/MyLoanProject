RBI Regulations & Loan Compliance

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Loan Regulations

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) sets strict guidelines to ensure transparency, fairness, and accountability in all lending activities. Every bank and financial institution must comply with these standards before approving or disbursing loans. Below are the major RBI rules and compliance areas that govern the loan approval process:

1. Fair Practices Code (FPC)

Banks must follow RBI’s Fair Practices Code while processing loan applications. This ensures fair treatment, transparency in interest rates, and timely communication of decisions to borrowers.

2. KYC (Know Your Customer) Norms

All customers must undergo KYC verification by submitting identity and address proofs. This prevents fraudulent loans and money laundering activities.

3. Credit Information Reporting

Banks are required to report loan data to credit bureaus like CIBIL, Experian, and CRIF Highmark, which helps in assessing an individual’s repayment history and creditworthiness.

4. NPA (Non-Performing Assets) Guidelines

Loans that remain unpaid for over 90 days are classified as NPAs. RBI requires banks to follow a structured recovery plan for such accounts.

5. Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

RBI mandates that a portion of total lending (usually 40%) must go to sectors like agriculture, education, housing, and MSMEs to promote financial inclusion.

6. Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

RBI regulates the maximum amount a bank can lend against collateral. This ensures that banks don’t take excessive risk while financing secured loans.

7. Interest Rate Transparency

All interest rates must be clearly disclosed using the Marginal Cost of Lending Rate (MCLR) or external benchmarks. No hidden charges are allowed.

8. Customer Grievance Redressal

Banks must provide an easy-to-access complaint redressal mechanism for loan-related issues, as per RBI Ombudsman Scheme guidelines.

9. Data Privacy & AI Usage

AI-based credit scoring or loan decision systems must comply with RBI’s data privacy and algorithm transparency policies to protect borrower information.

10. Digital Lending Guidelines (2022)

RBI recently introduced new rules for digital lending apps, ensuring direct fund transfers between bank accounts and requiring prior approval for AI loan systems.
